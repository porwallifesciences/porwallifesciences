About Porwal Life Sciences

Rooted in India. Ready for the World.

Porwal Life Sciences is a merchant export company founded by Bhavya Porwal, based in Indore, India. We specialise in sourcing standardized botanical extracts and certified organic herbal powders from India and supplying them to international markets.

We focus on responsible sourcing, consistent quality, transparent documentation, and dependable supply to help international buyers source Indian botanical ingredients with confidence.

We believe good business is built on honesty and consistency. Our goal is to establish Porwal Life Sciences as a trusted Indian partner for botanical ingredient sourcing — one reliable order at a time.

Why Porwal Life Sciences

What Sets Us Apart

We are not a catalogue supplier. Every order is sourced, verified, and documented to the standards serious international buyers require.

01
Complete Batch Documentation

Every shipment includes a full COA covering heavy metals (Pb, As, Cd, Hg), EtO-free declaration, pesticide residue panel, microbiology (TPC, E.coli, Salmonella, Yeast & Mold), and assay results. No missing reports.

02
Verified Active Marker Content

Each extract is standardized for its specific active compound — withanolides for ashwagandha, curcuminoids for turmeric, boswellic acids for boswellia. Confirmed analytically on every batch.

03
Certified Manufacturing Partners

We source from manufacturers holding relevant certifications including ISO, GMP, and FSSAI. Their credentials are shared with buyers openly — you know exactly where your product was made.

04
Trial Orders Welcome

Start with a small sample or a 25 kg trial order. Our suppliers support trial quantities with full documentation so you can evaluate quality before committing to volume.

05
Export-Ready from Day One

Products packed in export-grade HDPE drums and food-grade bags. Phytosanitary certificates, EtO-free declarations, non-irradiation statements, and allergen declarations arranged per shipment.

06
Direct & Responsive

You deal directly with us — no intermediary layers. Since we coordinate with manufacturing partners on each inquiry, please allow 48 hours for a detailed response with specs, COA samples, and pricing.
